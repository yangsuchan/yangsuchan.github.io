Efficient learning / Data selectionJul–Nov 2026

ShallowFrontier: Energy-Efficient LLM Data Selection via Asymmetric Prefix Extrapolation

How can we select useful instruction data while reducing the cost of selection itself? This project builds on an InstructDiff reproduction to study efficient, domain-agnostic data selection.

10%of training data used
53%lower GPU energyvs. InstructDiff, 3 rounds
+0.21average score differencevs. each setting’s best InstructDiff round

Team project results across four experimental settings. Performance uses three-seed averages; GPU energy is compared with InstructDiff’s three-round run.

Research focus

Investigated domain bias in entropy-based selection and the mismatch between sample-based selection in the paper and token-budget selection in the implementation.

Method

After one calibration, group examples by question representations. Use group-level gradient noise estimates to choose a selection rule and allocate the data budget.

Experimental findings & research lessons

Compared with full-data training, the project reports 61% lower mean GPU energy across the four settings. In a representative mixed-domain experiment, selected medical examples increased from 2 to 203, with MedQA scores changing from 35.27 to 57.03.

Reproduction work highlighted the need to verify that sampling units and experimental budgets match the paper before interpreting a comparison.

Language models / Post-trainingAug–Nov 2026

Persona-Consistent Language Model Post-Training

Building a language model that maintains a persona’s characteristics across multi-turn conversations, using Character-LLM-style experience reconstruction.

My contribution

Automated the persona data construction and training pipelines, tracing the workflow from character profiles through generated dialogue to SFT data. Applied QLoRA-based SFT and subsequent preference alignment with DPO.

Evaluation

The SFT model achieved a mean Neeko evaluation score of 6.52. After DPO, qualitative evaluation suggested improved persona consistency and response stability.

What the evaluation revealed

Qualitative inspection identified factual hallucinations, persona contradictions, and off-topic responses after SFT. Preference data paired more persona-consistent responses with less consistent alternatives. Follow-up qualitative evaluation examined persona consistency and response stability.
